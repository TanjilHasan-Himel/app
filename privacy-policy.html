OVERVIEW Audia Player is designed as a local-first Android audio player. In the version described by this policy, core library, playback, favorites, playlists, and schedule functions operate on the device without a Developer-operated account or cloud service. The App is not designed to sell or profile your listening history. The disclosures below must match the actual App build, including any third-party software components. 2. INFORMATION ACCESSED AND PROCESSED ON YOUR DEVICE With the permissions available to it, the App may read media information such as audio file names, titles, artists, albums, durations, artwork, and file locations or identifiers to display and play local audio. It may store local preferences, favorites, playlists, listening counts, filters, and scheduled playback settings. These records are intended for on-device functionality and are stored in App-managed storage. The App does not require an account, email address, or telephone number for its core local player functions. 3. DATA SENT TO THE DEVELOPER The local-player version described here does not intentionally upload your audio files, file paths, playlists, listening history, or schedule details to a Developer-operated server. It is not configured to use Developer-operated user analytics, advertising networks, or remote crash-report collection. This statement applies only after verification of the released App and all included third-party libraries. Android, your device manufacturer, the app store, and any enabled device backup service may independently process device or diagnostic information under their own policies. 4. PERMISSIONS AND PURPOSES The App may request or use the following, as applicable to your Android version and selected features: • Music/audio access: discover and play audio available on the device. • Foreground media playback: continue eligible audio playback while the App is in the background and display playback controls. • Notifications: show playback-related controls and notices when supported and permitted by the system. • Alarms and reminders special access: attempt to trigger user-configured scheduled playback at requested times; Android may require separate approval. • Device startup or wake-related access: only if implemented, to restore eligible scheduling behavior after a restart or during sleep. Permissions may be denied or changed in Android Settings. Denial can limit the related feature. The App should not request access unrelated to an enabled feature. 5. LOCAL STORAGE, BACKUP, AND DELETION Preferences, playlists, favorites, listening counts, and scheduling configurations are normally stored in the App's local storage. Clearing App data or uninstalling the App usually removes its local private data from that device. However, Android or device-manufacturer backup and transfer systems may preserve or restore certain App data, depending on your settings and the App's backup configuration. The Developer does not have a server-side copy of the local library in the version covered by this policy. Original audio files stored outside the App remain under your control and are not ordinarily deleted by uninstalling Audia Player. 6. SECURITY The App relies on Android application isolation and platform permission controls for access to locally stored data. No software or device can be guaranteed completely secure against loss, unauthorized access, malware, or hardware failure. Protect your device with a secure screen lock and keep important audio and settings backed up through a method you trust. 7. ADVERTISING, ANALYTICS, AND THIRD-PARTY SERVICES The local-player version described here does not intentionally show third-party advertisements or include a Developer-operated behavioral analytics service. If a future update introduces Firebase, Crashlytics, online lyrics, music identification, streaming, social functions, cloud sync, or any other online service, this policy and any required in-App disclosures or consent mechanisms must be updated BEFORE enabling that functionality. Information sent to external providers and their retention practices will be described at that time. 8. CHILDREN The App is a general-purpose local audio player and is not specifically designed or marketed to children. The Developer does not knowingly solicit children's personal information through a registration system. Parents or guardians should manage device permissions and the content available to children. Any distribution specifically directed at children will be reviewed for additional applicable legal and store requirements. 9. YOUR CHOICES AND CONTROL You can manage the App's media and notification permissions in Android Settings, review or remove locally stored playlists and schedules where the App supports this, clear App storage, or uninstall the App. These actions may remove local preferences or stop related functions. For questions about privacy or data handling, contact the address below. 10.
